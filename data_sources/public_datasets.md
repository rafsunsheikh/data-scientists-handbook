# Public Datasets

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## What to cover

- General catalogs: Kaggle, HuggingFace Datasets, Google Dataset Search, AWS Open Data, Data.gov, EU Open Data.
- Government / official statistics: US Census, BLS, BEA, Eurostat, OECD, IMF, World Bank, FRED.
- Health: NHANES, BRFSS, MIMIC-III/IV, UK Biobank.
- Geospatial: OpenStreetMap, Natural Earth, GADM, SEDAC.
- Climate / Earth: NOAA, NASA EOSDIS, Copernicus, ERA5, MODIS.
- Computer Vision: ImageNet, COCO, Open Images, LAION-5B.
- NLP: Common Crawl, Wikipedia, Project Gutenberg, OSCAR.
- Audio: AudioSet, LibriSpeech, VoxCeleb.
- Domain-specific: Stack Overflow dumps, GitHub Archive, Twitter / X academic, IMDb, MovieLens.
- Licensing — many public datasets are *not* permissive; document carefully.
- Reproducibility — version your downloads; sources can disappear or change.
