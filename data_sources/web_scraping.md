# Web Scraping

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## What to cover

- Static HTML: `requests` + `beautifulsoup` / `lxml` / `selectolax`.
- Dynamic content: headless browsers (`playwright`, `selenium`).
- Sitemaps and robots.txt as a starting point.
- Crawling frameworks: `scrapy`.
- Ethics: terms of service, copyright, rate limits, identifying yourself.
- Anti-bot countermeasures: rotating proxies, residential IPs, fingerprinting — and the legal limits.
- Parsing pitfalls: encoding, JavaScript-rendered content, infinite scroll, lazy-loaded images.
- Structured data on pages: schema.org JSON-LD, OpenGraph tags.
- Archiving for reproducibility: WARC, Wayback Machine, internal cache.
- Maintenance: scrapers break — monitor, alert, version your selectors.
