# Sensors and IoT

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## What to cover

- Industrial sensors: temperature, pressure, vibration, flow.
- SCADA / PLC / Historian systems (OSIsoft PI, Aveva Historian).
- Protocols: OPC-UA, MQTT, Modbus, BACnet.
- Wearables: accelerometer, gyroscope, PPG, ECG; sample rates, drift.
- Smart-home: Zigbee, Z-Wave, Matter; rates and reliability.
- Edge processing: feature extraction on-device, partial aggregation.
- Time synchronization (NTP, PTP) — sensor drift across a fleet.
- Calibration: drift over time, environmental compensation.
- Missing data patterns: connectivity loss, sensor failure.
- Storage: time-series DBs (InfluxDB, TimescaleDB) or partitioned Parquet.
- Privacy: location, identity, biometric data implications (HIPAA, GDPR).
