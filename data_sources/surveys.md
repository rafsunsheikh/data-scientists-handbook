# Surveys

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## What to cover

- Survey types: cross-sectional, longitudinal/panel, repeated cross-sections.
- Sampling: probability vs. convenience; stratified, cluster, snowball.
- Weighting and post-stratification.
- Question design: wording effects, ordering, scales (Likert, NPS), open vs. closed.
- Modes: face-to-face, phone, online, mail; mode effects on response.
- Non-response: bias, follow-ups, imputation.
- Common biases: social-desirability, acquiescence, response-set, satisficing.
- Privacy and consent (IRB, GDPR, HIPAA).
- Public survey datasets: General Social Survey, World Values Survey, BRFSS, NHANES.
- Tools: Qualtrics, SurveyMonkey, REDCap, LimeSurvey, Typeform.
- Analysis: design-aware statistics (`survey` in R, `samplics` in Python).
