# Manufacturing

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## Highlights to cover

- **Data shapes:** sensor time series (vibration, temperature, pressure, current), SCADA / PLC tags, MES / ERP, quality measurements (SPC), bills of materials, work orders, yield, traceability, vision-system images.
- **Sources:** OPC-UA, OSIsoft PI, Aveva Historian, Siemens TIA, MES platforms.
- **Cleaning particulars:** tag-name normalization across lines; downsampling from kHz sensor data; calibration drift; gap-filling during downtime; multi-rate alignment.
- **Standard analyses:** predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, root-cause analysis, yield optimization, SPC and process capability (Cp, Cpk), design-of-experiments (DoE).
- **Visualizations:** control charts (X̄/R, EWMA, CUSUM), Pareto charts (top defect causes), heatmaps of machine × shift performance, Sankey of process flows.
- **Regulation:** ISO 9001, IATF 16949 (automotive), 21 CFR Part 11 (pharma), FDA validation, EU MDR (med devices).
- **Public datasets:** Bosch Production Line Performance (Kaggle), NASA Bearings, CMAPSS turbofan degradation.
- **References:** Montgomery. *Statistical Quality Control.* Box/Hunter/Hunter. *Statistics for Experimenters.*
