## Highlights to cover

- **Data shapes:** EHR (structured + free text), claims, lab results, vitals time series, imaging (DICOM), waveforms (ECG, EEG), genomics, wearables / RPM, clinical notes, registry data.
- **Standards:** ICD-10/11, CPT, SNOMED CT, LOINC, RxNorm, HL7 v2, **FHIR**, DICOM.
- **Sources:** Epic, Cerner, Athena, Allscripts; payer claims (CMS, commercial); clinical trial CRFs; biobanks (UK Biobank); wearables (Apple Health, Fitbit API).
- **Cleaning particulars:** code-system mapping; units of measure normalization (mg/dL vs. mmol/L); date / encounter alignment; identifying continuous vs. ambulatory measurements; cohort construction.
- **Standard analyses:** disease prediction, readmission, sepsis early warning, length-of-stay, claims fraud, population health, cohort survival.
- **Visualizations:** Kaplan-Meier curves, forest plots, swimmer plots, calendar heatmaps of encounters.
- **Regulation:** HIPAA (US), HITRUST, GDPR + national laws (EU), 21 CFR Part 11 (FDA), de-identification (Safe Harbor, Expert Determination), IRB approval for research.
- **Public datasets:** MIMIC-III/IV, eICU, NHANES, BRFSS, CDC WONDER, SEER, UK Biobank (gated).
- **References:** Friedman & Hripcsak. *Biomedical Informatics: Computer Applications in Health Care and Biomedicine.* OHDSI / OMOP CDM documentation.
