# Energy and Utilities

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## Highlights to cover

- **Data shapes:** smart-meter consumption (AMR/AMI), grid sensor / PMU data, weather, generation by source (solar, wind, hydro, fossil), market prices (day-ahead, intraday), outage records.
- **Sources:** SCADA, ISO/RTO data (CAISO, ERCOT, NYISO, MISO, ENTSO-E), Copernicus, NOAA.
- **Cleaning particulars:** meter rollovers, daylight-saving, weather alignment (nearest station vs. interpolation), unit conversions (kWh vs. MWh).
- **Standard analyses:** load forecasting (short and long term), renewable generation forecasting, demand-response targeting, grid anomaly detection, asset-failure prediction.
- **Visualizations:** load duration curves, heatmaps (day × hour), generation stack charts.
- **Regulation:** NERC CIP, FERC, regional regulators, GDPR for consumption data, EU CER directive.
- **Public datasets:** UCI Individual Household Electric Power Consumption, GEFCom load forecasting, ENTSO-E Transparency Platform, EIA Open Data.
