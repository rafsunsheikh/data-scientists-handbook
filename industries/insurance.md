# Insurance

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## Highlights to cover

- **Data shapes:** policies, exposures, premiums, claims (FNOL, claim notes, payments, reserves, recoveries), customer / KYC, third-party (credit, motor vehicle records, weather, geospatial peril maps).
- **Lines of business:** P&C (personal auto, home, commercial), life, health (overlap with healthcare), specialty (cyber, marine).
- **Cleaning particulars:** policy versioning (endorsements, mid-term changes), exposure-on-risk vs. earned-premium alignment, claim development triangles, peril coding, geocoded loss vs. address quality.
- **Standard analyses:** pricing / GLM rating models, reserving (chain-ladder, Bornhuetter-Ferguson, stochastic), CAT modeling, fraud detection, retention / lapse.
- **Visualizations:** loss-ratio heatmaps, claim-frequency × severity scatter, development triangles, geo loss accumulation maps.
- **Regulation:** state DOIs (US), Solvency II (EU), IFRS 17, NAIC model laws, anti-discrimination rules on rating factors.
- **Public datasets:** Kaggle Porto Seguro, French motor third-party liability (`freMTPL`), AllState claim severity.
- **References:** Frees. *Regression Modeling with Actuarial and Financial Applications.*
