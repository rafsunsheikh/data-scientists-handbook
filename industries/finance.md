## Highlights to cover

- **Data shapes:** market data (tick, OHLCV bars, order book L1/L2/L3), reference data (instruments, corporate actions), transactions / orders, positions, P&L, risk factors, customer (KYC), credit, fundamentals.
- **Standards / protocols:** FIX, ISIN/CUSIP/SEDOL, ISO 20022, MIC, GICS, CFI, LEI.
- **Sources:** exchanges, Bloomberg, Refinitiv, Polygon, Quandl, internal OMS/EMS, core banking, card networks.
- **Cleaning particulars:** corporate-action adjustments (splits, dividends, mergers), survivorship bias, point-in-time data (avoid look-ahead), timezone alignment across exchanges, handling of after-hours, missing fundamental data revisions.
- **Standard analyses:** factor models, risk attribution, VaR / CVaR / ES, backtesting, transaction-cost analysis (TCA), fraud detection, credit scoring, AML, churn.
- **Visualizations:** candlestick + volume, depth book, drawdown curves, P&L heatmaps, correlation matrices.
- **Regulation:** MiFID II, Dodd-Frank, Basel III/IV, GDPR + national, PCI DSS, SOX, FATCA, CRS, FCRA (credit), Reg BI.
- **Public datasets:** FRED (macro), Yahoo / Stooq (daily), Lobster / TAQ samples (microstructure, gated), Kaggle competition data.
- **References:** Hasbrouck. *Empirical Market Microstructure.* Joshi. *The Concepts and Practice of Mathematical Finance.* López de Prado. *Advances in Financial Machine Learning.*
