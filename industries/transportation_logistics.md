# Transportation and Logistics

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## Highlights to cover

- **Data shapes:** GPS / AVL traces, AIS (maritime), ADS-B (aviation), shipment events, fleet telematics, capacity / load factor, on-time performance, fuel.
- **Standards:** GTFS / GTFS-realtime (transit), AIS NMEA messages, EDI 856/214 (logistics).
- **Sources:** vehicle telematics (Geotab, Samsara), AIS aggregators (MarineTraffic), ADS-B (FlightAware), TMS, warehouse WMS.
- **Cleaning particulars:** map matching GPS to road network, dropout filling, stop / trip segmentation, geo-fencing, identifying ETAs vs. actuals.
- **Standard analyses:** ETA prediction, route optimization, demand forecasting, last-mile delivery optimization, dwell-time analysis.
- **Visualizations:** flow maps, OD matrices, isochrones, dwell heatmaps.
- **Regulation:** hours-of-service (FMCSA), GDPR for driver / vehicle data, customs / sanctions screening.
- **Public datasets:** NYC Taxi & Limousine, Uber Movement, OpenStreetMap, GTFS feeds, Marine Cadastre AIS.
