# Government and Public Sector

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## Highlights to cover

- **Data shapes:** administrative records (tax, benefits, licensing), census, vital statistics, public-safety records (crime, calls for service), educational attainment, environmental monitoring.
- **Sources:** central statistical agencies (US Census, Eurostat, ONS, INSEE), Open Data portals, FOIA releases.
- **Cleaning particulars:** weighting / post-stratification, missing-by-design fields, geography changes over time (redistricting, county changes), language and code-list translations.
- **Standard analyses:** policy evaluation, program impact, equity analyses, demand forecasting (services, transit), risk targeting (within strict fairness constraints).
- **Visualizations:** choropleths, small-multiple time series across regions, accessible (color-blind, text alternatives).
- **Regulation / ethics:** statutory privacy rules, FOIA / sunshine laws, fairness and transparency obligations, freedom-of-information vs. minimization.
- **Public datasets:** virtually everything in this area is public — start with the agency's own portal.
