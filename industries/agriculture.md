# Agriculture

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## Highlights to cover

- **Data shapes:** satellite imagery (Landsat, Sentinel, planet), drone (RGB, multispectral, thermal), soil sensors (moisture, EC, pH, NPK), weather, yield monitor, machinery telemetry, market prices.
- **Sources:** ESA Copernicus, USGS, USDA, FAO, Climate Engine, John Deere Operations Center.
- **Cleaning particulars:** atmospheric correction of satellite imagery, cloud / shadow masking, sensor calibration, georeferencing alignment between data layers, fitting growing-season windows.
- **Standard analyses:** yield prediction, crop classification, disease / pest detection, irrigation scheduling, variable-rate prescription, pricing.
- **Visualizations:** NDVI / EVI time series, field maps, growing-degree-day curves.
- **Regulation:** crop insurance reporting, country-specific subsidy data privacy, environmental compliance.
- **Public datasets:** Cropland Data Layer (USDA), Sentinel via Copernicus, FAOSTAT, CHIRPS rainfall.
