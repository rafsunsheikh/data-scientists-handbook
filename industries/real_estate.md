# Real Estate

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## Highlights to cover

- **Data shapes:** MLS listings, transactions (deeds), tax assessments, property attributes (bed/bath/sqft), parcels, photos, geospatial (zoning, schools, transit), rents, occupancy.
- **Sources:** local MLS feeds, county recorder data, CoreLogic, Zillow Z-trans (historical), OpenStreetMap, Census.
- **Cleaning particulars:** address parsing & standardization, lot vs. property unit, dedup across listings (same property relisted), tracking time-on-market, controlling for vintage and renovations.
- **Standard analyses:** automated valuation models (AVM), market-rent estimates, rental yield, lead scoring, portfolio risk, mortgage delinquency.
- **Visualizations:** price heatmaps, days-on-market charts, comparables grid.
- **Regulation:** fair housing (Fair Housing Act, ECOA in US), GDPR, anti-discrimination in pricing models.
- **Public datasets:** Zillow Research, FHFA HPI, US Census ACS, NYC Open Data property data.
