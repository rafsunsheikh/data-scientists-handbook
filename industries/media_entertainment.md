# Media and Entertainment

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## Highlights to cover

- **Data shapes:** streaming sessions (play / pause / seek), content metadata, ratings / reviews, recommendations / impressions / clicks, ad impressions, subscriber lifecycle.
- **Sources:** CDN logs, player telemetry, CMS, content rights systems, ad servers (DFP / GAM, OpenRTB exchanges).
- **Cleaning particulars:** session stitching across devices, bot filtering, content-ID normalization across licensing windows.
- **Standard analyses:** recommendation systems, churn prediction, content valuation, A/B testing of player and UI, ad pricing.
- **Visualizations:** retention curves by content, engagement funnels, content embedding maps.
- **Regulation:** GDPR, COPPA (kids content), national broadcasting rules, content moderation regulations.
- **Public datasets:** MovieLens, Netflix Prize (historical), Spotify Million Playlist, IMDb dumps.
