# Education

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## Highlights to cover

- **Data shapes:** LMS clickstreams (Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard), assessment outcomes (formative, summative), attendance, enrollment, course catalog, demographic, outcomes (graduation, employment).
- **Sources:** SIS (Student Information Systems), LMS, standardized testing providers, state longitudinal data systems (SLDS).
- **Cleaning particulars:** identity resolution across systems (LMS userid vs. SIS), academic-year alignment, course-level vs. assignment-level granularity, accommodating dropouts and transfers.
- **Standard analyses:** at-risk identification (early warning systems), retention / completion, learning analytics (knowledge tracing), curriculum effectiveness, equity gaps.
- **Visualizations:** funnel from enrollment to graduation, cohort heatmaps, item-response curves, equity dashboards with disaggregated metrics.
- **Regulation:** FERPA (US), GDPR + national, COPPA for K-12, accreditation reporting (IPEDS).
- **Public datasets:** IPEDS, Kaggle student-performance datasets, OULAD (Open University Learning Analytics).
