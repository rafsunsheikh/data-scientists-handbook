# Marketing and Advertising

> **Stub — contributions welcome.**

## Highlights to cover

- **Data shapes:** ad impressions, clicks, conversions, audience attributes, campaign metadata, spend, web sessions, CRM, MMP (mobile measurement) events.
- **Sources:** ad platforms (Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn), DSPs, MMPs (AppsFlyer, Adjust), web analytics (GA4), CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot), Segment / CDP.
- **Cleaning particulars:** identity stitching across cookies / devices / mobile IDs in the post-IDFA / post-cookie world, view-through vs. click-through attribution, fraud / bot traffic, currency, time-zone alignment for global campaigns.
- **Standard analyses:** attribution (last-click, time-decay, Markov, MMM/media mix modeling, incrementality), uplift modeling, audience segmentation, frequency capping, A/B testing.
- **Visualizations:** funnel + drop-off, MMM contribution charts, geo lift maps, attribution comparison.
- **Regulation:** GDPR, ePrivacy / cookie consent, CCPA / CPRA, COPPA, Apple ATT, Google Privacy Sandbox.
- **Public datasets:** Criteo conversion logs (sample), Avazu, Outbrain Click Prediction (Kaggle).
