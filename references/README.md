# References

A starter bibliography for the broader topics covered in this handbook. Each industry chapter lists its own references at the end.

## Foundational textbooks

- **Wickham, H. & Grolemund, G.** *R for Data Science*, 2nd ed. (free online) — `https://r4ds.hadley.nz/`
- **VanderPlas, J.** *Python Data Science Handbook* (free online) — `https://jakevdp.github.io/PythonDataScienceHandbook/`
- **Hastie, T., Tibshirani, R., Friedman, J.** *The Elements of Statistical Learning* (free PDF).
- **Bishop, C.** *Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning*.
- **Goodfellow, I., Bengio, Y., Courville, A.** *Deep Learning* (free online) — `https://www.deeplearningbook.org/`
- **Murphy, K.** *Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction* (free online).
- **Hyndman, R. & Athanasopoulos, G.** *Forecasting: Principles and Practice*, 3rd ed. (free online) — `https://otexts.com/fpp3/`

## Data visualization

- **Tufte, E.** *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information*.
- **Wilke, C.** *Fundamentals of Data Visualization* (free online) — `https://clauswilke.com/dataviz/`
- **Cairo, A.** *The Truthful Art*.
- **Knaflic, C. N.** *Storytelling with Data*.
- **Munzner, T.** *Visualization Analysis and Design*.

## Causal / experimental

- **Imbens, G. & Rubin, D.** *Causal Inference for Statistics, Social, and Biomedical Sciences*.
- **Pearl, J., Glymour, M., Jewell, N.** *Causal Inference in Statistics: A Primer*.
- **Kohavi, R., Tang, D., Xu, Y.** *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments*.

## Data engineering and quality

- **Kleppmann, M.** *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*.
- **Reis, J. & Housley, M.** *Fundamentals of Data Engineering*.
- **Wickham, H.** *Tidy Data* — Journal of Statistical Software 59(10).
